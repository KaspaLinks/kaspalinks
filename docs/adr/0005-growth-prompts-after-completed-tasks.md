# Growth Prompts appear only after completed tasks

KaspaLinks invites visitors to create their own profile only after they finished something real:
a confirmed payment or a successfully claimed Claimable Link. The prompt has one call to action,
stays hidden for signed-in Creators, and carries a fixed `utm_source` label instead of cookies or
client-side tracking. Payers may share the creator's public page; claim pages never offer sharing
or read the location, because the claim key lives in the URL fragment.
